Cognitive Bias

Avoiding the Gambler's Fallacy

A team on a 5-game skid is never "due" for a win. Each fixture is an independent event. Learn to base forecasts strictly on empirical matchups rather than streaks.

Patience & Selective Action

The Importance of Patience

Avoid the urge to bet every game on the slate. Patiently waiting for one exceptional, high-edge value setup outperforms ten low-confidence wagers every time.
