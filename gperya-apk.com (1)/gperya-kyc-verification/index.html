⚠️ Common KYC Rejection Causes & Fixes

Name Mismatch Error

The full name on your Gperya account must match your government ID and GCash/Maya name character-for-character. Contact support if you need to correct typos.

Blurry or Glared Images

Flash glare or camera blur prevents automated document scanners from reading ID text. Take photos in a well-lit room on a dark neutral surface.

Clipped Corners

Ensure all four corners of your physical ID card are visible in the photo frame. Do not crop out margins or borders.

Expired Documents

Expired identification cards are automatically rejected by compliance screeners. Always submit valid, unexpired credentials.
