What Information to Prepare

Before reaching out, prepare the details that help our team understand your proposal: your name, company or channel name, website and social links, audience location, monthly traffic or engagement context, primary content topics, proposed campaign idea, preferred timeline, contact email, and any disclosure or compliance requirements your organization follows.

You do not need a finished media kit to start a conversation, but organized information makes review easier. Screenshots, rate cards, audience summaries, sample content, and previous campaign examples can be helpful when they are available.

Partnership Guidelines

Partnership content should be accurate, transparent, and appropriate for the audience. Partners should avoid unsupported guarantees, misleading claims, hidden disclosures, or pressure-based messaging. Any approved collaboration should clearly explain its purpose and should respect applicable platform rules, audience expectations, and responsible gaming standards.

We may decline proposals that do not fit the brand, lack clear ownership, rely on low-quality traffic, or conflict with user trust. A careful review process protects both sides and helps keep long-term collaborations sustainable.
